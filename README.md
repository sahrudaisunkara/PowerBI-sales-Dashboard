# 📊 Power BI Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard** built using **Power BI** to analyze sales trends, subcategory performance, profit distribution, and regional insights.
The dashboard helps stakeholders quickly understand key business metrics and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze total sales, profit, and quantity metrics.
* Identify top-performing product subcategories.
* Understand sales distribution by segment and category.
* Visualize geographic sales patterns across regions.
* Provide clear insights through interactive visualizations.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Information

The dataset contains sales transaction data including:

* Product categories and subcategories
* Sales and profit values
* Quantity sold
* Customer segments
* Regional and state-level information

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

### ✅ KPI Summary Cards

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Quantity

### 📊 Sales by Subcategory

* Pie chart showing sales contribution across product subcategories.
* Helps identify highest revenue-generating products.

### 📉 Sales Performance Analysis

* Bar chart comparing sales across subcategories.
* Highlights strong and weak performing areas.

### 🧩 Segment & Category Analysis

* Donut charts representing:

  * Sales by customer segment
  * Sales by category

### 🗺️ Regional Sales Map

* Geographic visualization displaying sales distribution by state and region.

### 💰 Profit Analysis

* Profit comparison across subcategories using bar visuals.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop**
* Data Modeling & Transformation
* Interactive Filters & Slicers

---


## 🚀 Key Insights

* Tables and Phones contribute significantly to total sales.
* Certain subcategories generate higher profit margins.
* Sales distribution varies across regions and customer segments.
* Visual analytics improves understanding of performance trends.

---

## ▶️ How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file.
2. Open it using **Power BI Desktop**.
3. Interact with filters and visuals to explore insights.
